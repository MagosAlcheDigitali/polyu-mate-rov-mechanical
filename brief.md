# PolyU MATE ROV End-Effector Goal Brief

**Goal:** Develop a prototypeable, serviceable ROV end effector for PolyU's 2027 MATE Explorer product-demonstration tasks. Keep the user's vehicle concept as integration context; do not claim fit until its body CAD and mission-prop interfaces are checked.

## Current design direction

One forward-facing powered pivot jaw with a fixed blunt reaction/push face, blunt hook, and mechanically retained replaceable pads. The fixed and moving pads default to matching round channels, with independent round/V/flat profile settings for fit studies. ROV motion provides approach and axial insertion. Do not design a task-specific insert, actuator rating, or final mount from assumptions in place of the actual props/body.

Current OpenSCAD concept defaults: 24 mm open jaw gap, 0-44 degree commanded sweep, about 3.18 mm analytic hard-close gap, 95 x 103.5 x 112 mm study envelope, and 97.5 mm forward reach from its mount datum. These are study values, not released fabrication dimensions. The integral Ø4 mm stop pin has only about 2.0 mm sector-slot engagement and about 0.5 mm arm-root overlap; the current custom-drive stop geometry is not approved for powered use.

A separate `gripper_study_stop_integrated_candidate.scad` copies the current gripper study and adds an integrated double-supported Ø8 mm cross-pin stop, curved sector slot, D-profile shaft/fork interface, bridge and guard proxies. Sampled intersections for sector/fork, bridge-post/fork, bridge-plate/fork, and guard/fork were empty at 0°, 22°, and 44°; the source compiles with OpenSCAD `--hardwarnings`. The bridge joints and vehicle load path are still not demonstrated, and actuator, retention, materials, strength, guard access, vehicle fit, and prop clearance remain open. This is a separate unpowered packaging candidate; `gripper_study.scad` remains unchanged and retains its unsuitable printed stop.

The earlier integrated candidate's generic actuator-side coupling uses a round bore. The newer jointed candidate adds a parametric D-shaft proxy. Blue Trail's current SER-203x/SER-213x manufacturer materials specify a 6 mm titanium D-shaft; a separate 70 x 32 x 80.1 mm package envelope is available in `bte_ser203x_package_proxy.scad` for early overlay only. It omits connector and exact hub/side-hole geometry and does not establish fit. The optional `show_ser203x_package_proxy` assembly view shows the real-size case against the generic mount: the current Ø28.5 x 6 mm reaction ring occupies the same volume as the SER housing for 6 mm axial length, and the Ø34 mm actuator shroud is too small for its coaxial footprint. The Ø84 mm stop enclosure has only 3.5 mm nominal corner-radius margin before cable/connector. Redesign the mount/guard from vendor STEP before considering this drive a fit candidate; no drive is selected. At the manufacturer's 5.4/5.9 N·m stall torque figures, lossless 35.5 mm pad-arm calculations produce upper-bound stall-force screens of about 152/166 N; the 23 mm stop-radius screens are about 235/257 N. BTE speed figures are no-load values. These figures are not loaded grip-force or continuous-torque ratings. See `actuator_selection_basis.md` for the source-backed limits.

The separate `gripper_study_stop_jointed_candidate.scad` adds visible screw/washer/nut and insert proxies at the bridge-to-bearing, bridge-post-to-sector, and stop-block-to-sector joints, plus an optional motor D-shaft coupler. Its assembly and coupler compile warning-free; seven intersection pairs (including each retained-fastener group) are empty at 0°, 22°, and 44°. This is static packaging evidence only. The joint design, stop contact, strength, materials, guard access, hardware fit, and body/prop integration remain open. The main `gripper_study.scad` remains unchanged.

The separate `gripper_study_stop_guard_enclosure_candidate.scad` adds a 360° wall, removable lid, and two lower panels. Selectable STLs are available for shell, left/right floor panels, lid, and hardware proxy. The latest OpenSCAD validator passed hard-warning compiles, empty floor/support/bearing/actuator/bridge/shaft and shell/floor intersections, and guard/mechanism checks at 11 samples from 0° through 44°. The lower panels use Ø7.3 mm boss reliefs with Ø9 mm underside screw-head flanges; the shell flange starts 0.2 mm above the panel plane. Ø2.5 mm printed pilots assume self-tapping screws and need material-specific coupon/pullout testing. The 12.4 mm cheek port is only nominally 0.1 mm below the MATE opening dimension, and physical access/fit are unverified. See `guard_enclosure_candidate_review.md`. The active `gripper_study.scad` remains unchanged.

**Actuator/file distinction:** `gripper_study.scad` does not use a selected servo. Its Ø20 x 45 mm body and Ø6 mm shaft are generic packaging placeholders only. The separate `sg90_quick_gripper.scad` is a minimal low-load mockup driven by an SG90 servo horn; it is not the actuator package for the main pivot jaw and does not establish wet-use suitability.

The replaceable-pad keeper screw stations now scale with pad length from their 24 mm reference pattern, with a warn-only check for crowded screw heads. This makes coupon/profile revisions less error-prone but does not size screw engagement, pad material, retention strength, or loaded jaw hardware.

The keeper/pad rail has been widened to 12 mm, and the adapter plate and rear cross-tie are now both 84 mm wide in the study. A Tappex Trisert-3 M3 316 stainless insert is represented as a fit candidate, using the manufacturer's approximate plastic pilot range and boss dimensions. Print the separate `pad_retention_coupon_body.stl`, `pad_retention_coupon_pad.stl`, and `pad_retention_coupon_keepers.stl` to assemble the fit coupon with real hardware; `pad_retention_coupon.stl` is only the visual assembly with hardware proxies. Manufacturer dimensions require application verification; insert retention in the team's actual polymer and process is not known.

The nominal Ø8 mm pivot-groove study now uses the Rotor Clip DSH-8 DIN 471 reference values (Ø7.6 mm groove root, 0.9 mm minimum width, 0.8 mm ring thickness). The modeled 11 mm ring remains a simplified annulus without the catalog lugs or full retaining-ring envelope; recheck the selected ring, washer/guard fit, groove material, and load capacity before machining or loading the shaft.

## Goal completion gates

1. Dimension and collision-check against the team's body STEP/Parasolid and actual thruster/camera geometry.
2. Dimension the jaws and contact features against official prop CAD/drawings or faithful physical practice props.
3. Measure wet retention, safe squeeze, push/pull/insertion forces, travel, and required cycle time.
4. Select a real depth-rated actuator and complete its power, torque reaction, shaft-retention, guarding, and mounting interfaces.
5. Validate a physical prototype through bench and wet task trials; resolve inspection/safety review.
6. Release an updated, fabrication-ready design package. The public GitHub repository is [`MagosAlcheDigitali/polyu-mate-rov-mechanical`](https://github.com/MagosAlcheDigitali/polyu-mate-rov-mechanical). Its public `main` branch contains the project documents and all 43 STL outputs: 40 direct STL blobs plus three full meshes in a nine-part recovery bundle. The three truncated blobs were removed. The vehicle STEP/STP assembly and practice-prop geometry are still outstanding.
